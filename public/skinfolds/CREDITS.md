# Skinfold reference images

## Triceps and subscapular photographs

- Files: `triceps.jpg`, `subscapular.jpg`
- Source: National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES), Anthropometry Procedures Manual, January 2007. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Center for Health Statistics.
- URL: https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nhanes/public/2007/manuals/manual_an.pdf
- Figures: Exhibit 3-13, triceps skinfold measurement. Exhibit 3-16, subscapular skinfold measurement.
- Licence: Public domain. This is a work of the United States federal government and is not subject to copyright in the United States. The copies in this folder are cropped and compressed from that manual.

## Sites without a usable free photograph

Chest, midaxillary, abdominal, and thigh are original line diagrams drawn for Spotter. Searches that did not turn up a usable free image of the site itself:

- Wikimedia Commons categories and file search for skinfold, skin fold caliper, plicometria, suprailiac, and Hautfalte. Results were calipers, unrelated skin folds, or journal PDFs, not site photographs. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Skinfold_caliper
- Openverse, licences limited to CC0, public domain, and CC-BY, for “skinfold caliper”, “suprailiac skinfold”, and “plicometria”: no results.
- NHANES 2007 anthropometry manual: photographs of the triceps and subscapular folds only.
- NHANES III (1988–94) manual: line figures for triceps, subscapular, and suprailiac. The thigh skinfold is described, and Exhibit 3-3 shows seated upper-leg length rather than the standing anterior fold, so it was not used for the thigh.
- U.S. Army ANSUR measurer’s handbooks (1988 and 2010–2011), approved for public release: illustrated landmark manuals, not skinfold-site photographs.
